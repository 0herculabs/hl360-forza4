# FM4 v0.4.0 - Native PC Port (Windows x64)

## Changes since v0.3.9

- Rebuilt on the ReXGlue SDK v0.10.0 with this project's SDK patches. The game renders through Direct3D 12 again.
- Racing wheels: any USB device can be bound through a guided Wheel & Pedals tab, with a live view of axes and buttons, native force feedback tuning and wheel rotation settings.
- SimHub telemetry over UDP (FM7 "Dash" or Horizon format).
- Wide screens (21:9, 32:9): Fill Window (Hor+), Black bars or Stretch, with the HUD kept in the middle or moved to the window's edges.
- Live camera adjustment: field of view and seat position per camera view, from the keyboard or wheel buttons, saved to `fm4.camera.toml`.
- Additional content in the Install tab: the installed Disc 2 packs and DLC are listed with their names and sizes, and each can be removed. DLC packages can be added from a folder (for example a 4D530910 folder copied from a console) or as package files. Only FM4 downloadable content is installed; avatar items and other games' content are left out.
- Portuguese in the game language selector (issue #28).
- The launcher window can be resized and opens no larger than the screen (issue #29).
- Fixed a crash after about 11 minutes of play caused by running out of import thunks.
- The module-reload fix for leaving a circuit is now part of the SDK runtime instead of a separately pinned DLL.
- Kept from v0.3.9: graphics presets, VSync toggle, desktop restoration on exit, audio output selection and Forza Plus 120 FPS physics switching.

Wheel, telemetry, wide-screen and camera work by Ross Carlson (pull requests #21 and #23).
