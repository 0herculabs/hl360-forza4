# FM4 v0.4.0 - Native PC Port (Windows x64)

## Changes since v0.3.9

### Graphics and performance
- Updated the Vulkan renderer to a newer ShiftGlue SDK revision (the one fm4-static-recomp uses), now built reproducibly from source.
- Renderer settings now follow values verified for FM4 on this SDK:
  - Shaders compile before they are drawn, so the road no longer appears dark or grey for several seconds.
  - Less CPU work per frame.
  - A smaller texture memory budget.
- New **Race detail** settings in the Graphics tab, applied when you press Play:
  - Car detail: Console, High or Ultra.
  - Reflection updates: Console, Every race or Double.
  - Full-rate mirror with up to 16 cars, full-rate bloom, soft particles, cone and barrier shadows, and crowds in every race.
  - An experimental option to turn exterior car shadows back on.

### Frame rate and pacing
- Fixed menus and car presentations running at double speed.
- In 120 FPS mode, the menus and the showroom camera move at their real speed.
- Frames are paced by a single clock. A second limiter could repeat or drop a frame every few seconds.
- The launcher now explains that VSync disables G-Sync/FreeSync. With a variable refresh rate display, turn VSync off.

### Racing wheels, telemetry and camera (Ross Carlson, pull requests #21 and #23)
- Bind steering, pedals, buttons and an optional H-shifter from any USB devices, with a live view of their input.
- Force feedback tuning and wheel rotation settings.
- SimHub telemetry over UDP.
- Live camera adjustment: field of view and seat position for each camera view, from the keyboard or wheel buttons.
- Wide screens (21:9, 32:9) with Fill Window, Black bars or Stretch. HUD placement on the Vulkan renderer is still being tested, and the HUD "Edges" mode currently has no effect with it.

### Launcher
- **Additional content** in the Install tab:
  - Lists the installed Disc 2 packs and DLC with their names and sizes, and removes any of them.
  - Adds DLC from a folder (for example a 4D530910 folder copied from a console) or from package files.
  - Avatar items and other games' content are skipped.
- Portuguese is available as a game language (issue #28).
- The window can be resized and never opens larger than the screen (issue #29).
- New icon for the game and the launcher. The windows are titled "Forza Motorsport 4" and "Forza Motorsport 4 Launcher".

### Fixes
- Includes the module-reload fix for the crash when leaving a circuit, which the v0.3.9 build lacked.
